\subsection{Development on past cohorts}
\begin{outline}
  \item Built and tuned on four earlier offerings: 119 teams with prompted team-dynamics
    journals.
  \item Consistency: run-to-run agreement on the per-round flags was 86--96\% over 433
    team-rounds.
  \item Consistency is not correctness: the flags were stable but the supporting quotes
    were not. In one cohort, 69\% of raised flags came back with different quotes across the
    three runs, and a few quotes contradicted their own flag. This led to the
    word-for-word evidence rule.
\end{outline}

\subsection{Use on a live round}
\begin{outline}
  \item Ran the three-flag tool on journal 3 of the deployment semester (43 teams, three
    runs each).
  \item Each of the six tutors received a build covering their own teams
    \todo{confirm: own teams?} and read the journals inside it.
  \item Task 1, before seeing any flags: for each team, ``seems fine / keep an eye on /
    step in''.
  \item Task 2, flags hidden: for each snippet (with surrounding text), pick which of the
    three problems it shows, ``none'', or ``can't tell''.
  \item Controls mixed in: 212 snippets = 135 flagged by the tool + 40 benign passages the
    tool never flagged + 37 passages all three runs agreed on.
  \item No action was taken on the tool's output during the semester.
\end{outline}
