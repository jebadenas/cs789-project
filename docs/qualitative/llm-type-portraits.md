# Cascade-type portraits (from the blind marks)

**Date:** 2026-09-01. Companion to llm-results.md.

Each cascade state's **distinctive signature** — features elevated/depleted
*relative to the other types* (not just the base rate) — plus a representative
grounded example (names scrubbed to member labels). Descriptive, not inferential:
cells are small (n=8–48), so read these as **portraits**, not tests. Divergence =
count of 9 imbalance/conflict features (0–9).

---

### Silent-flat (n=10) — the textbook team · divergence 0.0
**Elevated:** harmonious 100%. **Depleted:** under-contribution 0%, sub-group 0%,
unaddressed 0% — everything.
Fair effort, no standouts, no friction, start to finish. The pure well-functioning
team. *"Working well together, fair effort, no major conflict, whole-project
involvement."*

### Silent-lone-dissenter (n=10) — harmony with a pocket of dissent · divergence 2.0
**Elevated:** open conflict 30% (the *only* elevated feature). **Depleted:**
sub-group, communication breakdown.
Effort is shared and there's no imbalance — but one member voices open disagreement
(over direction, grades, tech choices). Friction over **ideas**, not contribution —
a lone dissenter, exactly as the name implies. *"Open conflict — [a member]
expressing frustration about his grade and suggesting changes to the tech stack."*

### Contested (n=8) — calm on the surface · divergence 1.9
**Elevated:** harmonious 75%. **Depleted:** under-contribution, unaddressed.
Despite the peer-raters *disagreeing about who's good* (what "Contested" means in the
ratings), the journals read as calm and balanced — **no visible dysfunction.** The
rater disagreement does **not** surface in the journals. Its defining trait is the
*absence* of a signature — the standout candidate for the (2) deep-dive.
*"Consistently worked well together, fair effort distribution, no major conflicts."*

### No standout (n=48) — the unremarkable middle · divergence 2.3
**Elevated:** nothing. **Depleted:** communication breakdown (slightly).
The modal team, sitting at the overall average on every feature — ordinary ups and
downs, no defining dysfunction or exceptional harmony. It is the baseline the other
types are read against.

### One at bottom (n=26) — the quiet under-contributor · divergence 3.0
**Elevated:** under-contribution 73%, communication breakdown 38%, sub-group 50%.
**Depleted:** harmonious.
One member drifts or goes missing; the rest form a working core and route around the
gap, with some coordination friction. A slow fade, not a blow-up. *"[Member C] was
missing for several weeks and did not contribute much."*

### One at top (n=8) — friction around an over-carrier · divergence 4.5
**Elevated:** effort imbalance 62%, under-contribution 88%, communication breakdown
50%. **Depleted:** harmonious.
Work is lopsided (one/some carry, others lag) **and** there's coordination friction —
a more turbulent imbalance than One-at-bottom. *"Some team members did not contribute
as much as others."*

### Both ends (n=8) — the silent split · divergence 4.6 (most divergent)
**Elevated:** singled-out-below 75%, sub-group 88%, under-contribution 100%.
**Depleted:** open conflict **0%**, harmonious 12%.
The team cleaves into a carrying core and a clearly-named dead weight — but with
**no open conflict.** A silent freeze-out / work-around, not a fight: the most
divergent type, yet the calmest surface. *"[A member] is consistently mentioned as
not contributing, especially Weeks 6–7 and 10–11."*
